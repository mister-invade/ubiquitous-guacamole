Passive Perception // Stealth

Two systems that trip up nearly every new player.

Passive Perception

What it is: a baseline "always on" awareness score — 10 + your Wisdom (Perception) modifier. The DM uses it instead of asking you to roll.

When it's used: noticing something you're not actively looking for. You never roll for this — it's compared silently against a DC.

Stealth

What it is: an active roll (Dex (Stealth) check) when you try to hide, sneak, or move unseen.

How it's checked: your roll is compared against whoever might notice you's passive Perception. Beat it, and they don't spot you.
